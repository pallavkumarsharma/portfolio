Integration Modernisation for Informa Global

Redesigning legacy middleware patterns for one of the world's largest B2B information and events companies — delivering ~15% operational cost reduction and reusable assets that accelerated every subsequent integration.

Context

Informa operates at significant scale — a global portfolio of B2B brands, events, and digital products. Their integration landscape reflected years of organic growth: point-to-point connections between systems, inconsistent patterns, and middleware that had been extended beyond its original design intent.

The cost of this complexity was measurable: high maintenance overhead, slow time-to-change, and integration failures that required manual intervention to resolve.

Approach

The modernisation work focused on three areas: pattern standardisation, asset reusability, and operational simplification.

Pattern standardisation meant identifying the 80% of integration patterns that repeated across the landscape — data synchronisation, event-driven updates, bulk file processing — and establishing canonical implementations for each. Instead of every integration solving the same problem differently, these patterns became building blocks.

Asset reusability meant extracting the common elements — error handling flows, retry logic, transformation utilities, monitoring hooks — into a shared library. New integrations inherited these components rather than reimplementing them.

Operational simplification meant consolidating monitoring and alerting onto a single observability surface, and documenting the operational runbooks that support teams needed when things went wrong.

Outcomes

The ~15% operational cost reduction came from two sources: reduced maintenance overhead on existing integrations (fewer incidents, faster resolution when incidents did occur) and faster delivery of new integrations (reusing existing assets rather than building from scratch).

The governance framework we put in place was probably the most durable output — it gave Informa a way to make integration decisions consistently, rather than case-by-case.
